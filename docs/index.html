FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is virtual staging legal for MLS listings?

Yes! Virtual staging is widely accepted. Simply disclose that photos are virtually staged, which is standard practice and required by most MLS systems.

What photo quality do you need?

We recommend at least 2000x1500 pixels. Most smartphone photos work great. Better input quality = better staging results.

Can you stage furnished rooms?

Yes! We offer "virtual decluttering" to remove existing furniture first, then restage with new pieces. This is priced as an add-on service.

What's your refund policy?

If you're not satisfied after revisions, we offer a full refund. No questions asked. We've never had to issue one though!

How does AI staging compare to manual?

Our AI produces results indistinguishable from manual staging, but faster and cheaper. We also have human QA review on every image.

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes! Our Enterprise plan starts at $12/photo. Contact us for custom pricing for brokerages doing 100+ photos/month.
