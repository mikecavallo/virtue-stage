## Market Position Strategy

**Position**: Premium AI at mid-market prices

- **vs Manual competitors** (BoxBrownie, Stuccco): Same quality, 10x faster
- **vs Budget AI** (Virtual Staging AI): Better quality (human QA), same speed
- **vs Premium** (VS Solutions): 80% cheaper, faster delivery

## Recommended Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Starter - $24/photo
**Target**: Occasional users, try-before-you-buy
- Standard quality staging
- 24-hour delivery
- 1 revision included
- 5 furniture styles
- Email support
- **Margin**: ~85% ($21 profit)

### Tier 2: Professional - $16/photo ⭐ ANCHOR
**Target**: Active agents (5+ listings/month)
- Premium quality staging (AI + Human QA)
- Same-day delivery (under 8 hours)
- Unlimited revisions (30 days)
- 20+ furniture styles
- Priority support
- Bulk upload (up to 50 photos)
- **Margin**: ~82% ($13 profit)

### Tier 3: Enterprise - $12/photo
**Target**: Brokerages, photography studios
- Everything in Professional
- 2-hour rush delivery
- API access
- White-label options
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom style creation
- Volume commitments required (100+/month)
- **Margin**: ~75% ($9 profit)

## Launch Pricing Strategy

### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Month 1-2)
- Launch at **$12/photo** (Professional tier)
- Goal: Build reviews and testimonials
- Limit: 1,000 photos
- Create urgency: "Founding member pricing"

### Phase 2: Standard Pricing (Month 3+)
- Move to **$16/photo** standard
- Grandfather early adopters at $12
- Introduce Enterprise tier

### Phase 3: Premium Features (Month 6+)
- Add Starter tier at $24 (captures price-insensitive)
- Add premium add-ons
- Introduce annual prepay discounts

## Key Pricing Principles

1. **Anchor on Professional** - Most users should land here
2. **Enterprise locks in volume** - 12-month commitments
3. **Starter captures surplus** - Some will pay more for simplicity
4. **Never compete on price alone** - Speed + quality differentiate
5. **Transparent pricing** - No hidden fees, what you see is what you pay
