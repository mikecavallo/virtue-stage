## Business Summary

### The Problem
- Physical staging costs $1,500-$5,000+ per home
- Takes days to schedule and set up
- Only viable for luxury listings
- Empty homes sell slower and for less

### The Solution
AI-powered virtual staging that:
- Costs $16/photo (95% cheaper than physical)
- Delivers same-day (vs 48hr industry standard)
- Works on any listing (vacant, furnished, construction)
- Includes human QA for quality guarantee

### Market Opportunity
- **Market size**: $150-250M US (2025), growing 15-25% CAGR
- **Target**: 1.5M real estate agents in US
- **Unit economics**: 80-85% gross margin
- **Year 1 target**: $2-5M ARR

## Key Insights from Research

1. **AI is disrupting the market** - Prices dropping from $75 to under $3/photo
2. **Speed is underserved** - No one offers reliable same-day at scale
3. **Quality concerns with pure AI** - Opportunity for AI + human QA hybrid
4. **Enterprise market open** - Few competitors offer API access
5. **Physical staging is dying** - 90%+ cost savings make virtual staging inevitable
